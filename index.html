1. What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering attack where attackers impersonate trusted organizations via email, messaging, or fake websites to trick victims into revealing sensitive information like login credentials or credit card numbers.

Common Indicators (Red Flags):

  • Mismatched Sender Domains: The sender's email address does not match the official organization domain (e.g., support@paypa1-security.com instead of paypal.com).
  • Urgent or Threatening Language: Creating artificial urgency (e.g., "Account suspended within 24 hours!").
  • Generic Greetings: Using "Dear Customer" instead of your name.
  • Suspicious Links: Hyperlinks pointing to unfamiliar external URLs.

2. Interactive Quiz: Spot the Attack

Review the sample email mockup below and evaluate whether it is legitimate or a phishing attempt.

From: IT Helpdesk <security-alert@company-net-login.com>
To: user@company.com
Subject: URGENT: Password Expiration Notice

Dear Valued Employee,

Your network password is set to expire in 2 hours. To maintain access to your email and portal, you must verify your credentials immediately.

Click Here to Update Your Password Now

Thank you,
IT Security Team

3. Best Practices to Prevent Phishing

  1. Inspect Links: Hover over links before clicking to inspect the real target URL.
  2. Verify Senders: Cross-check email headers and sender domains carefully.
  3. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): MFA blocks unauthorized logins even if credentials are compromised.
  4. Report Suspected Emails: Use your organization's "Report Phishing" button immediately.
